## What I Do

- **Enterprise platforms** — core systems with hundreds of forms, strict access control, and audit that survive a decade without rewrites.
- **Legacy modernization** — migrating critical systems without downtime, with rollback measured in minutes, not hours.
- **Data & AI platforms** — from data lake and ML execution frameworks to production AI in dozens of enterprise systems.
- **MCP-ready systems** — making enterprise backends navigable and tool-accessible for LLM agents, without tool bloat.

## Selected Results

- Designed and shipped a **banking low-code platform** (forms, metadata, access, audit) that supported **200+ forms and 80k DAU** and lived **10 years without a rewrite**.
- Migrated a **legacy queue management system** to a modern platform for **51,000 employees** without stopping the business. Rollback: minutes.
- Built a **Data/AI platform** and deployed a model execution framework into **22 enterprise systems**, including full MLOps lifecycle.
- Shipped a **recommendation system** to a customer mobile app at production traffic.
- Built and launched a **product-constructor** for iOS and Android, reaching mass distribution.
- Created a **test automation platform for AI agents**, used by engineers and business users to run scenarios and measure outcomes.
- Designed and deployed a **management reporting platform** for a territorial bank: KPI tree, responsibility matrix, financial integration.
- Built a **simulation model of a branch network** (Java) answering "how many and which staff to add" to hit queue SLAs.
- Implemented an **MCP server** with menu-based dynamic navigation and context-aware tools — solves tool bloat in enterprise systems with hundreds of forms.
- Managed **budget and infrastructure for DS teams** (compute, data lake, marts), enabling data delivery and model training across several divisions.
